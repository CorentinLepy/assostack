# Partnerships and sponsorships

AssoStack models partnerships as an operational role attached to existing CRM Contacts. A partnership record never becomes a second company or person database.

## Identity boundary

The canonical partner is always a `contacts` record. Most sponsors and suppliers will use `Contact(kind=organization)`, while the generic core also permits an individual Contact for patrons or person-to-person partnerships.

The optional `primaryContact` is another Contact in the same organization and represents the operational interlocutor. Company names, postal addresses, email addresses and personal identity remain owned by CRM Contacts.

## Partnership levels

`partnership-levels` is a tenant-scoped reusable taxonomy for tiers such as Main partner, Gold, Silver or Supporter.

Each level has a required human-readable name, a normalized tenant-local key, an active/archive lifecycle, an optional non-negative integer `sortOrder`, and an optional concise description. Keys are unique inside one organization but may be reused by another tenant.

A referenced level must be archived rather than deleted so historical partnership records remain intelligible.

## Partnerships

`partnerships` records the lifecycle of a specific deal, season or relationship. Multiple partnership records may therefore reference the same Contact.

A partnership contains a human-readable name and tenant-local key, required partner Contact, optional primary Contact and level, kind (`sponsor`, `partner`, `supplier`, `institutional`, `media`, `other`), lifecycle status (`prospect`, `negotiating`, `active`, `paused`, `ended`, `declined`), optional start/end dates, optional provider-neutral agreement/external references, optional staff note and immutable `createdBy` attribution.

When both dates are present, `endsAt` cannot precede `startsAt`.

## Tenant isolation and access

Payload multi-tenancy scopes both collections by `organization`. Server-side hooks additionally validate every relationship so crafted cross-tenant IDs are rejected even when the admin UI is bypassed.

Organization admins and editors may create/read/update partnership data. Deletion is restricted to organization admins. Ordinary member users do not receive broad staff-side access in this version.

## Database invariants

The PostgreSQL schema must enforce the same important invariants as application hooks:

- unique `(organization_id, key)` for partnership levels;
- unique `(organization_id, key)` for partnerships;
- check constraint preventing `ends_at < starts_at` when both partnership dates are present;
- foreign keys and useful tenant/relationship/status/date indexes generated by Payload.

Application-level validation remains necessary to provide clear API errors and to protect tenant relationship boundaries before database writes.

## Deliberate non-goals

This first boundary does not model accounting, invoice recognition, monetary valuation, contract document workflows, e-signatures, public sponsor logo rendering, automated renewals, campaigns, a sponsor portal or Team SMH-specific racing/paddock branding.

Those capabilities can layer on top of the stable generic partnership source of truth later.
